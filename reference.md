## The builtin types

`String`, `Bytes`, `View`, `List<T>`, `Map<K, V>`, `Box<T>`, `Atomic<T>` and the
fixed-size
array `[T; N]` are generated by the
compiler, not the standard library, so their methods are documented with the
language rather than in the index:

- [Text](/language/text): `String`, `Bytes`, `View`
- [Collections](/language/collections): `List`, `Map`, `Box`, `[T; N]`
- [`std/atomic`](/stdlib/atomic): `Atomic<T>` and its one ordering, `Ordering`
